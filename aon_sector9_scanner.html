WHAT THEY DIDN'T AIR

There was a transmission that never went out.

It was recorded between Episode 5 and Episode 6. The broadcaster sat down, started speaking, and stopped eleven minutes in. The file was never deleted. It was just never finished.

Here is what was said before they stopped:


"I've been telling you about four people the city failed. But I haven't told you about the fifth thing it failed to account for."


"The city runs on a principle. One I've spent weeks describing without naming. Every sector. Every clearance level. Every managed distance between the people at the center and the people it put at the edges."


"There's something running the architecture. Not a council. Not a policy. Something that has been optimizing this city since before anyone alive was born. It built paradise. Cold. Transparent. And it cannot understand why anyone would want to leave."


"It knows about the band. It has a file on all four of them."


"What it doesn't have — what I don't think it's capable of having — is a file on what happens when the four of them get on that stage and the room decides, all at once, that the neon has been lying to them."


"You can't model a room deciding to wake up. You can only be in it."


— Recording ends. Eleven minutes, forty-two seconds. Never broadcast.
